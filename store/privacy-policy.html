Opening Quest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Opening Quest ("the app") is a chess training app made by Opening Quest. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect or store your personal data on any server.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (XP, levels, stars, achievements, streak, rating), your settings and the games you played in the app are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or using "Reset progress" in Settings, deletes them.

Chess.com game import (optional)

If you choose to import games, the app sends the Chess.com username you type directly to Chess.com's public API (api.chess.com) over an encrypted HTTPS connection, to download that player's public games. This request goes from your device to Chess.com; it does not pass through any server of ours, and we do not receive or keep the username or the games. Chess.com handles the request under its own privacy policy: chess.com/legal/privacy. Opening Quest is not affiliated with Chess.com.

Pasted games (PGN)

Games you paste are analysed on your device by the built-in Stockfish chess engine. They are not uploaded anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos access.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Permissions

Internet: only used for the optional Chess.com import. Vibration: short haptic feedback during games (can be turned off in Settings).

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
